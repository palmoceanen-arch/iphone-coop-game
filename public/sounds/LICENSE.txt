Ambient layers (wind, water, forest hum, campfire crackle, crickets,
distant birds) are generated procedurally in `src/sound.js` from
shaped noise + low-frequency oscillators, so no looped audio file is
shipped for them. This keeps the bundle small and lets the ambient
texture vary continuously instead of repeating with a perceptible loop
seam.
