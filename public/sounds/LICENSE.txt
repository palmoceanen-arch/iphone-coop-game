The four enemy_voice_*.ogg files were normalised to roughly -18 LUFS
peak so they sit ~6 dB under the hit_flesh impact (which leads on the
combat-hit voice mix in src/sound.js). Source clips were re-encoded to
mono, 44.1 kHz OGG Vorbis q=4 to match the rest of the bundle.

The qubodup whoosh files were re-encoded from their original CC0
preview MP3s on freesound.org to OGG Vorbis (mono, q=4, 44.1 kHz) so
they match the rest of the bundle's encoding.

Ambient nature layers split into sample-based and procedural:
  wind / water / fire   — the three `ambient_*.ogg` files above, played
                          as looping AudioBufferSourceNodes (see
                          src/sound.js:_buildAmbient).
  forest / birds /      — generated procedurally in src/sound.js from
  crickets                shaped noise + scheduled chirps. No looping
                          recording reads as a generic windless
                          leaf-rustle hum or a sparse meadow chorus
                          without locking the soundscape to a specific
                          real-world location.
