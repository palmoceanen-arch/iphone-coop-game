PhysBall Arena

Casual 2D multiplayer physics football. Arrow keys + Space, or touch joystick on mobile.

How to play
  • Move: WASD / Arrow keys, or left-side virtual joystick on mobile.
  • Kick: Space / X / Enter, or the right-side kick button on mobile.
  • Push the ball into the opposing team's goal. First to 5 wins.
  • You're auto-assigned to the smaller team on join.
